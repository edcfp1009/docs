title: "Quoting"
description: "Compare rates and available carriers."
---

All quoting tools are **read-only**. Requesting a quote does not book a shipment or incur charges.

## `get_rate_quotes`

Returns rate quotes from available carriers based on origin, destination, weight, freight class, and other shipment details.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Does not book a shipment.** To book, use [`create_shipment`](/tools/shipping).
- **Example prompt:** _"Get me rate quotes from Mission Viejo, CA to Dallas, TX, 500 lbs, class 70."_

## `get_carriers`

Returns the list of carriers available to your FreightPOP company account.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Example prompt:** _"Which carriers do we have set up in FreightPOP?"_
