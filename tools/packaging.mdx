## `get_packaging_rules`

Calculates recommended shipment packaging based on the origin and destination addresses plus the line items being shipped.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Example prompt:** _"What packaging should I use for 4 pallets going from Lake Forest, CA to Dallas, TX?"_

Use this before requesting a quote or booking a shipment if you're not sure how the line items should be packaged.
