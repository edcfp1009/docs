---
title: "Tracking"
description: "Look up shipment status, history, and lists."
---

All tracking tools are **read-only**. They retrieve data from your FreightPOP account and never modify anything.

## `get_shipment_status`

Returns the current status, last tracking event, and estimated time of arrival for a specific shipment.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Example prompt:** _"What's the status of shipment PRO123456?"_

## `get_shipment_history`

Returns the full tracking event log for a shipment — every scan, hand-off, and status change from pickup through delivery.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Example prompt:** _"Show me the full tracking history for shipment PRO123456."_

## `list_shipments`

Returns a list of shipments filtered by date range and/or status.

- **Type:** Read-only
- **Example prompt:** _"List all shipments that are in transit from the past 7 days."_
