Shipping tools are **write** tools. They create real records and can charge your FreightPOP account.

## `create_shipment`

Books a real shipment with a carrier through your FreightPOP account.

- **Type:** Write
- <Warning>
    **This tool books a real shipment and charges the account.** Your AI client will always ask for your explicit confirmation before calling `create_shipment`. Always review the carrier, service, and total charges shown in the confirmation before you approve.
  </Warning>
- **Example prompt:** _"Book the cheapest quote from that list with XPO."_

### What happens on success

- A shipment record is created in FreightPOP.
- The selected carrier receives the booking.
- Your account is charged per your normal FreightPOP billing terms.
- Tracking becomes available via [`get_shipment_status`](/tools/tracking) once the carrier posts the first event.
