The **FreightPOP MCP Server** connects your FreightPOP account to AI clients like Claude and ChatGPT using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Once connected, you can look up shipments, request rate quotes, import orders, and book shipments by asking your AI client in plain English, without switching tabs or digging through menus.

## Who this is for

FreightPOP customers who want to run day-to-day freight operations through an AI client (Claude Desktop, Claude.ai, ChatGPT) instead of navigating the FreightPOP web app. If you already have a FreightPOP account, you can connect it in a few minutes.

## What you can do

Ask your AI client things like:

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Track a shipment" icon="location-dot">
    _"Where is order 82520261?"_
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pull up a shipment record" icon="file-lines">
    _"Show me the shipment for order 82520261, including the BOL."_
  </Card>

  <Card title="Get rate quotes" icon="dollar-sign">
    _"Quote 2 pallets, 400 lbs each, 48x40x48 inches, from Lake Forest, CA to Anaheim, CA."_
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Use a real tracking number, order number, or shipment ID from your own FreightPOP account. For quotes, give the pallet count, weight, and dimensions; FreightPOP works out the freight classification for you.

See [Available Tools](/tools/overview) for the full list of what the AI client can do on your behalf.
