The **FreightPOP MCP Server** connects your FreightPOP account to AI clients like Claude and ChatGPT using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Once connected, you can track shipments, request rate quotes, create orders, and book shipments by asking your AI client in plain English — no switching tabs, no digging through menus.

## Who this is for

FreightPOP customers who want to run day-to-day freight operations through an AI client (Claude Desktop, Claude.ai, ChatGPT) instead of navigating the FreightPOP web app. If you already have a FreightPOP account, you can connect it in a few minutes.

## What you can do

Ask your AI client things like:
