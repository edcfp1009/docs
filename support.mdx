## Response time

We aim to respond to MCP-related support requests within **one business day** (Monday–Friday, US Pacific time). Urgent production issues affecting live shipments are prioritized.

## Before you write in

A quick check of [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) resolves most common issues in under a minute. If you're still stuck, include:

- Your AI client (Claude Desktop, Claude.ai, ChatGPT)
- The prompt you sent
- The full error message shown by the AI client
- Approximate time and time zone of the request

That context helps us diagnose faster.
