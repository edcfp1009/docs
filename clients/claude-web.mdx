---
title: "Claude.ai (Web)"
description: "Connect FreightPOP to Claude.ai from the Claude connector directory."
---

FreightPOP is listed in the Claude connector directory, so adding it takes one click. No URL to copy, no configuration.

## Prerequisites

- A Claude.ai account on a paid plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise)
- An active FreightPOP account
- MCP access enabled for your user (ask your FreightPOP admin to turn on **AI Accessibility** for you and your company)

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the FreightPOP listing">
    <a href="https://claude.ai/directory/freightpopmcp" style={{ display: "inline-flex", alignItems: "center", gap: "8px", padding: "10px 20px", borderRadius: "8px", background: "#16A34A", color: "#FFFFFF", fontWeight: 600, fontSize: "15px", textDecoration: "none" }}>
  Add FreightPOP to Claude
</a>

    Or find it yourself: in Claude.ai, open **Settings → Connectors**, browse the directory, and search for **FreightPOP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the FreightPOP listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to FreightPOP">
    A popup opens to the FreightPOP login page. Sign in with your normal credentials and approve the requested permissions. The popup closes automatically when done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    FreightPOP appears in your connector list as **Connected**. Try a test prompt from [Getting Started](/getting-started).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding it manually instead

If you need to point Claude at the server directly rather than use the directory listing, add it as a custom connector with this URL:

```text
https://tms-mcp.freightpop.com/mcp
```
