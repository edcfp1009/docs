---

Set up the FreightPOP MCP Server as a connector in the web version of Claude at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).

## Prerequisites

- A Claude.ai account with Connectors enabled
- An active FreightPOP account

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Claude Connectors">
    Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and open **Settings → Connectors** (or visit the Connectors page directly from your profile menu).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the FreightPOP connector">
    Click **Add connector** and paste the FreightPOP connector URL:

    ```text
    https://tms-mcp.freightpop.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to FreightPOP">
    A popup window opens to the FreightPOP login page. Sign in with your normal credentials and approve the requested permissions. The popup closes automatically when done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    The FreightPOP connector appears in your connector list as **Connected**. Try a test prompt from [Getting Started](/getting-started).
  </Step>
</Steps>
