Find and connect FreightPOP through ChatGPT's Plugin Directory.

## Prerequisites

- A ChatGPT account (plugin availability can vary by plan, workspace, and region)
- An active FreightPOP account
- MCP access enabled for your user (ask your FreightPOP admin to turn on **AI Accessibility** for you and your company)

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plugin Directory">
    In ChatGPT, open the **Plugin Directory** (sidebar, **Settings → Plugins**, or the ChatGPT desktop app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find FreightPOP">
    Search for **FreightPOP** and open its listing to review what it does and what it can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. ChatGPT opens a popup to the FreightPOP login page. Sign in with your normal credentials and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    FreightPOP now appears as a connected app. Add it to a conversation with an @ mention, or from **\+ → More**, and try a test prompt from [Getting Started](/getting-started).
  </Step>
</Steps>
